Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers Desk Autopilot Pipeline, a private desktop tool that the owner of the Desk Autopilot YouTube channel uses to publish videos to that channel and read its statistics. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it. By using it, you agree to this policy.

YouTube API Services

Desk Autopilot Pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses and why

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth sign-in), the tool:

  • uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel, and edits their descriptions;
  • lists the channel's own videos and adds them to the channel's playlists;
  • reads the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers and estimated revenue).

Separately, with an API key and no sign-in, it reads public statistics (view counts and publication dates) of YouTube search results to estimate demand for video topics.

It does not access any other Google data, it does not collect information about viewers, and it never asks for or stores Google passwords.

How data is stored and shared

  • Everything is stored only on the owner's computer: the authorization token, the weekly analytics reports and the IDs of the channel's own videos and playlists.
  • Public statistics from search results are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • The tool checks the authorization every week. If it has been revoked or can no longer be refreshed, the tool deletes the token, the analytics reports and the stored video and playlist IDs (within 7 days at the latest).
  • Aggregated, anonymous channel statistics are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to write recommendations for the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with other third parties or used for advertising.

Cookies

The tool is a desktop program: it does not place, read or recognize cookies or similar technologies on any device or browser. This website does not use cookies or analytics either.

Deleting your data and revoking access

The owner can delete all stored YouTube data at any time with the tool's borrar-datos-youtube command. In addition, access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Contact

Questions or complaints about these privacy practices can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/deskautopilot/deskautopilot.github.io/issues.
