Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers Desk Autopilot Pipeline, a private tool that the owner of the Desk Autopilot YouTube channel uses to publish videos to that channel and read its statistics. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel;
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers and estimated revenue).

It does not access any other Google data, and it does not collect information about viewers.

How data is stored and shared

The authorization token and the analytics reports are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Aggregated, anonymous channel statistics may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to write recommendations for the owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to Google's handling of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the channel's About page.
